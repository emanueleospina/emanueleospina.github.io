About

I’m Emanuele — a Finance professional specialized in bridging the gap between quantitative analysis and financial structure.

My academic background is built on a double foundation. I hold a Bachelor's in Finance and Accounting — accredited by ACCA — which gave me a rigorous grounding in financial reporting, audit standards, and the mechanics of how businesses account for value. Alongside this, I completed an MSc in Finance with a specialisation in Quantitative Methods, where I developed the mathematical toolkit to model uncertainty, price risk, and analyse complex financial systems. I am currently deepening that foundation further with a degree in Computational Mathematics, driven by a genuine interest in the structures that underlie both pure mathematics and quantitative finance.

Currently based in Dublin, where I work in financial operations managing billing, reconciliation, and financial reporting across multiple client accounts. The role has reinforced the precision and attention to detail that I bring to any analytical work — while I continue building my quantitative foundation through my mathematics degree part-time.

My background spans financial audit and consulting across several European markets, which gave me a broad perspective on how financial systems operate in practice. That experience, combined with a growing interest in the mathematical foundations of finance, is what drove me to pursue a Mathematics degree alongside my professional career. My goal is to bring both sides together — the rigour of quantitative methods and the practical understanding of financial operations — in a role focused on risk, modelling, or quantitative research.

I'm naturally drawn to problems where structure matters — whether that's the logic of a consolidated balance sheet, the architecture of a derivative, or the Python code that connects them. This curiosity is what led me to pursue a degree in Computational Mathematics alongside my professional career: not as a detour, but as a deliberate effort to go deeper into the foundations that make quantitative finance rigorous.

Outside of the office, you’ll find me golfing, running, or following Real Zaragoza. I’m a firm believer that the discipline required for a long-distance run and the strategic patience of golf are essential traits for a well-rounded professional in high-pressure environments.
